About Me

AI-Native Full-Stack Developer

I'm Zaheer, based in Lahore with a BS in Software Engineering from UMT. My core expertise is frontend and full-stack development with React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js and modern databases, building applications that are responsive, scalable and genuinely user-friendly.
