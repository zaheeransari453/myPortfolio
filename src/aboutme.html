About Me

I'm Zaheer Ansari, an AI-Native Full-Stack Developer based in Lahore, with a BS in Software Engineering from the University of Management and Technology. I focus on building modern web applications, AI-powered products and intelligent digital experiences. My core expertise lies in frontend and full-stack development using React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js and modern databases, with a strong focus on creating responsive, scalable and user-friendly applications.

I have hands-on experience integrating REST APIs, LLMs, generative AI, RAG pipelines, vector search, AI agents and real-time voice AI into production-oriented applications. I enjoy working across the entire development lifecycle — from translating Figma designs into polished interfaces and building reusable components, to connecting APIs, databases, AI models and real-time services.

In practice that has meant shipping real products: Aurie AI, a meditation and wellness app running as both an Electron macOS desktop app and a Next.js web app at StrategistHub; RozeeGPT, an AI career assistant built with React and Material UI at Rozee.pk; and a personal multi-modal RAG platform that ingests documents and web pages and answers with cited, streaming responses and real-time voice.

I also follow an AI-native development approach, using tools such as Claude Code, Cursor and LLMs to accelerate coding, debugging, UI implementation and feature development. By connecting MCP servers with tools such as Figma, Slack, Linear, databases and other services, I build automated development workflows that reduce repetitive tasks and streamline the path from idea and design to implementation.

My goal is to combine strong software engineering fundamentals with modern AI capabilities to build products that are not only functional and scalable, but also intelligent, automated and enjoyable to use.

Outside of work I'm usually reading up on new tooling, gaming, or playing cricket. I'm always open to interesting projects — feel free to get in touch.
